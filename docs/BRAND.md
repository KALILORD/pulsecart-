# PulseCart — Brand Guide

## The idea

**PulseCart** is a trend-driven store. It watches what South Africa is talking about
today, finds the product behind the conversation, and puts it on a clean, fast storefront
before the wave peaks.

- **Name:** Pulse (the signal) + Cart (the purchase). Short, ownable, not geo-locked.
- **Tagline:** *What's trending. Delivered.*
- **Voice:** Sharp, confident, a little playful. Never spammy. We explain *why* something
  is trending, not just that it is.
- **Promise:** Curated, not crowded. A handful of trend picks at a time, each with a real
  reason to exist.

## Visual identity

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#101418` | Text, header, footer |
| Paper | `#F7F5F0` | Page background |
| Signal | `#FF4D2E` | Primary CTA, trend badges, links |
| Acid | `#D8FF3E` | Highlights, ticker, sale tags |
| Mist | `#E7E3DA` | Borders, muted surfaces |
| Slate | `#5B6169` | Secondary text |

- **Display type:** Space Grotesk — geometric, modern, slightly technical.
- **Body type:** Inter — neutral, highly legible.
- **Shape:** 16–24px radii, thin 1px borders, generous whitespace, editorial grids.
- **Texture:** Warm paper background, subtle grain, no glossy gradients.
- **Motion:** Quick and purposeful. 150–250ms ease-out. Respect reduced motion.

## Logo

The mark is a wordmark with a pulse stroke replacing the crossbar of the "t".
Files: `storefront/public/logo.svg`, `storefront/public/icon.svg`.

## Content rules

1. Lead with the trend, then the product.
2. One clear reason to buy in the first line.
3. Always show the landed ZAR price and delivery estimate.
4. Never invent stock, reviews, or scarcity. If it is a draft, it stays a draft.
5. Every post links back to the product page with UTM parameters.
